5 FAQs About Male Liposuction and Gynecomastia Surgery

What is male liposuction Manhasset NY patients ask about most?

Male liposuction is a surgical procedure that removes stubborn pockets of fat using small incisions and a thin tube called a cannula. Men in Manhasset most often request it for the abdomen, love handles, chest, and under the chin. It is meant for body contouring, not as a weight-loss method.

Am I a good candidate for male liposuction surgery?

Good candidates are generally in stable health, close to a healthy weight, and bothered by localized fat that has not responded to diet and exercise. Good skin tone and realistic expectations also help. A consultation is the best way to find out if the procedure suits you.

What causes gynecomastia, and can gynecomastia surgery fix it?

Gynecomastia is the enlargement of breast tissue in men, and it can result from hormonal changes, certain medications, weight gain, or no clear cause at all. When the tissue does not go away on its own, gynecomastia surgery can remove excess glandular tissue and fat to create a flatter chest. Your surgeon will review your history to confirm the cause.

How long is recovery after male liposuction or gynecomastia surgery?

Recovery varies by patient and by the areas treated. Many men return to desk work within several days to a week, and most need to avoid strenuous exercise for a few weeks. Swelling and bruising are normal and fade over time, and a compression garment is often recommended. You will receive personalized aftercare instructions.
